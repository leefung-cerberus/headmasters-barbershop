More than a barbershop

Where Craft
Meets Culture

Headmasters Beauty & Barbershop has been a fixture on Peachtree Road in Buckhead, where clients come for sharp results and a comfortable vibe. Modern sophistication, classic barbering, no attitude.

From skin fades and enhancements to straight-razor shaves, beard sculpting, braiding and specialty designs, every chair is run by a barber who treats the work like a craft. Walk in for a quick line-up or book the full signature service — you leave looking like the best version of yourself.

Services & Pricing

Standard-hours rates below. Book online or walk in — your barber will confirm the final price for specialty and combined services.

01 Cuts

Haircut $40 Clipper & scissor cut, styled. With beard / face detail — $50.
Shape-Up / Line-Up $30 Edge-up on the hairline and neck. With face detail — $40.
Kids' Cut — 10 & under $20 Ages 11 & up — $30.
Specialty Cuts & Hair Designs Varies Custom parts, patterns and freestyle line art. Priced by design.

02 Shaves & Beard

Razor Shave — Head $50 Hot-towel straight-razor bald shave. With face — $60.
Texturizer $60 Softening treatment for tighter textures.
Shampoo $10 Wash & rinse add-on.

03 Braiding & Designs

Braiding — Face frame from $50 Front / hairline braids.
Braiding — Full head from $60 Complete braid-down.
Braiding — Both from $80 Face frame + full head. Styles and length change the price.

04 Signature Packages

Ultimate Service $100 Full cut & style with straight-razor finish, facial and shampoo.
Supreme Service $80 Full cut & style with straight-razor finish plus facial or shampoo.

Prices are for standard business hours. After-hours appointments (roughly 9 PM to close) carry a different rate — ask your barber when you book. Specialty and combined-service pricing may vary.
